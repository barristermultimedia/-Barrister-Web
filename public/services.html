Questions

Frequently asked

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks is comfortable for most events. For larger productions, earlier is better. Short-notice jobs are possible, so ask.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel costs are written into the quote before you agree.

Can you stream to more than one platform?

Yes. We can send the same stream to several platforms at once and keep a recording.

What do I receive afterwards?

Edited video files, the raw recording if you want it, audio and print-ready design files, on the date we agree.

Do you add captions?

We can add captions and transcripts to recorded video on request, which helps viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing and people watching without sound.
